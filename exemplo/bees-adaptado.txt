City Bees

In many cities people keep bees now. Scientists look at these bees. City bees are
often stronger than farm bees. This sounds strange. But the reason is easy.

Big farms grow one plant only. All the flowers open at the same time. Then there
are no flowers for months. The bees have nothing to eat. A city is different. It
has gardens, parks and small green places. Flowers open there from spring to
autumn. Bees can travel from one garden to the next. They find food all year.

Farms also put poison on the plants. The poison stays in the flowers. The bees
eat it with their food. Cities use much less poison. Scientists looked at bees in
Berlin, London and Melbourne. The city bees had much less poison in them.

But there is a problem too. Some people put many bee houses on roofs. City
workers say this can be bad. Honey bees are farm animals. They are not wild. Too
many honey bees eat all the food. Then the wild bees find nothing. The
relationship between the two is not good. Wild bees often live alone. Many of
them eat only one kind of flower. For them this means death.

Scientists claim that more bee houses are not the answer. Wild bees need a good
home. People can plant local flowers. They can leave old wood on the ground.
They can cut the grass less. A wild garden is not always nice to look at. But
wild bees enjoy it. And they need it.
