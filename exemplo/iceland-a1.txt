Iceland Turns Carbon Into Stone

In Iceland there is a big machine. The machine takes air. In the air there is a
gas called carbon dioxide. This gas makes the world hot, so people want to take
it away.

The machine takes the gas out of the air. Then workers mix the gas with water.
They put the water deep under the ground, into the rock.

Something good happens there. The gas does not stay a gas. In about two years it
becomes stone. The stone is hard, and it stays under the ground for a very long
time.

The company says this is safe. The gas cannot come back up, because now it is
part of the rock.

But the machine is small. It takes only a little gas each year. People drive
cars and make things, and that puts much more gas into the air. So this machine
alone cannot fix the problem.

Still, many people think it is a good start. They want to build more machines,
and bigger ones, in other countries too.
