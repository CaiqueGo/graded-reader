{# The text read aloud by the browser's own speech synthesis. No audio file, no API, nothing sent anywhere: the voice is whatever English voice the operating system has. Good enough to start with, and free -- if it is not good enough for the ear, a TTS service is the next step, with its cost per character. The text is queued one sentence at a time. Chrome's online voices stop speaking a single long utterance partway through without any error, and a queue of short ones sidesteps that. Without an English voice it refuses to speak rather than fall back. The browser's fallback is the system's default voice, and on a Portuguese Windows that is a Portuguese voice reading English with Portuguese sounds -- worse than silence for someone learning how English sounds. Expects ``audio_text`` and ``audio_rate`` in the context. #}
